Sona on every page, and a sidebar that stays short at 1,000 patients

Round 2 answers two questions. First, what happens with 100 more patients: the sidebar never lists more than three people, and the Patients page uses urgency groups, saved views and an A–Z index. Second, Sona is now one ask bar in the same place on every page. It knows which page you're on, builds the answer as a view from the json-render catalogue, and can change the page or act for you after you review it.

Sona on every page

One ask bar that knows where you are and changes the page

The goal is fewer trips. Instead of opening the sidebar, finding a page and scanning a list, the clinician asks from wherever they are. The answer comes back as a small view built from the same parts the app uses, with its actions attached.

1 · Same place

Always at the bottom, always one tap

A glass bar with the Sona orb and a hold-to-talk button, in the same spot on every page. On review pages (Discharge, GL, X-ray) the orb sits beside the decision button, so it never covers the decision.

2 · Knows the page

A scope chip says what it's looking at

"Ward 4B", "Amelia Tan", "Your tasks". Questions are answered against that page's state. Tap × to ask across everything; the sidebar's "Search or ask" starts there.

3 · Suggests first

One ✦ suggestion per page, from live state

Above the bar: "Who can go home by noon?" on the ward, "1 overdue: give it to Nurse Aisyah?" on tasks. It's derived from the same rules as the page's insight line, so the two never disagree.

4 · Answers as views

Built from the catalogue, not free text

Each answer is a json-render spec made of app-owned parts (lead, takeaways, patient rows, metrics, review card). A "Composed from" line shows which parts, and the spec is one tap away for review.

5 · Changes the page

Apply, open or save the answer

Show on page filters the page with a removable ✦ chip. Open as page gives the answer a full page. Save to sidebar keeps it as a live view (at most three) that updates without asking again.

6 · Acts after review

A review card, then a receipt

"Give Siti's task to Nurse Aisyah" shows what will change, who is told and what stays the same, next to Confirm. After confirming, a receipt names what happened. Reading and filtering need no confirmation.

At 100 or 1,000 patients

Nothing lists everyone by default

The round-1 sidebar listed recent patients one by one, which breaks at 100. Now each surface shows a ranked slice with a count, and search or Sona reaches the rest.

Sidebar

  • Fixed length. Patients is one row with a count ("108 · 12 need you"). Continue shows 3 rows, then "See all 100 · 12 draft notes".
  • Search or ask at the top. A name, bed or MRN jumps straight to the patient; a question opens Sona across everything.
  • Saved views. Answers you save ("Home by noon") sit in their own group, at most 3, each with a live count.

Patients page

  • Views with counts: Needs you 12 · My list 18 · Ward 4B 32 · Clinic today 24 · All 108. It opens on Needs you, not the whole list.
  • Urgency groups: Needs you now, Next hour, Waiting, Done today. The top group shows 6 with "Show 6 more"; the big groups start folded with their counts.
  • All view: A–Z sections with an index scrubber on the right edge, and compact 44 pt rows.
  • Built on @legendapp/list, already installed, so 1,000 rows stay smooth.

Continue: where you left off

  • Who gets in, in order: the visit you have open, patients you pinned (5 at most), then unfinished work from the last 24 h (a draft note, a paused recording, an open next step).
  • Who leaves: a closed visit (note reviewed, discharged or handed over), an unpin, or 24 h without activity. Unsigned notes and unreviewed results become a task or a Today item first.
  • At 100: the Continue tab on Patients groups them by what's unfinished, oldest first, and offers to close the ones with nothing left after a review.
  • End of shift: "Wrap up" beside Hand over lists draft notes to sign and visits to close, so the list resets each shift.

Today and queues

  • Today shows the top 4 ranked items and "See all 12 that need you", never the full census.
  • Ward and Handover open on the filtered set (Blocked, To watch) with the others behind a segment.
  • Any list can be narrowed by Sona. "Who has K+ below 3.5?" turns 108 rows into 3 with a removable chip.
What's scattered

Five root causes

Each one produces several of the symptoms you noticed. Fix these and most pages improve without being redesigned.

1 · Sidebar

One flat list mixing four jobs

My work (messages, tasks), patient tools (Consultation, Notes), ward work (Ward, Handover) and admin (Audit, Training) all sit in one ungrouped list. Consultation and Notes need a patient, so they send you through the patient picker first. "Home" does nothing but close the drawer. "Share feedback" opens Settings.

2 · No page shape

Every page opens differently

There's no shared header and insight component (Sona Care has one: Title accent line=). Page titles use five different type sizes. Ward, Tasks, Handover, Note, X-ray and Consultation open with no answer to "where do I stand?".

3 · Duplication

The same thing in up to nine places

The patient list appears in 9 places, tasks in 9, and handover has 5 entry points. On the home screen alone, Messages appears as a key and as a top-bar badge. Settings and the sidebar both list recent encounters.

4 · Buried actions

The main action often sits at the bottom

Discharge, Guarantee letter, X-ray (Analyse), the patient sheet (Open visit, 4th block) and Training (Export) put the primary button below the review content. On a long page you scroll to act.

5 · Competing next steps

Four "next step" mechanisms in one visit

NeedLine, NextStepBar, "You might want to ask" and the Done-with keys all suggest what to do next, each with its own look. Five components render similar "needs attention" state.

Settings is a drawer of everything

"Your workspace" holds settings, the demo clock, developer interruption controls, the hospital library and recent encounters on one page of about 9 cards. Sign-in goes through the unstyled Better-T-Stack "Account scaffold".

Native title, matching the sidebar label

A short noun, the same word as the sidebar ("Messages", not "Patient messages"; "Settings", not "Your workspace"). The body never repeats it as an overline.

Insight line

One eyebrow of context, then one sentence in type.title with the part that matters in its meaning colour. It answers "does anything need me?" in 3 seconds. Empty states reassure: "Nothing needs you on the ward".

One feature card, with its action

Surface feature with the halftone glint, holding the single most important thing and its Action. Never more than one.

Ranked detail

Plain glass lists, ranked by urgency, every row with its reason. Secondary blocks fold to one line (Expand) and say what's inside ("3 beds free ›").

Quiet footer, or a sticky decision bar

Browse pages end with one caption (sample and demo labels merged). Review pages (Discharge, GL, Note, X-ray, Scan) pin the primary in a bottom bar with one "what happens" line above it, so the decision is never below the fold.
