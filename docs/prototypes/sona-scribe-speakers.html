Who said what, with any number of people in the room

Today the scribe knows two roles, clinician and patient, and guesses them from the words. This flow lets Sona tell voices apart by sound, however many there are, and asks the doctor only for what it cannot work out. The consultation below is synthetic.

    Rules this follows

    No fixed number of people

    Voices are numbered as they are heard. The planned model follows up to 8 at once; past that, the extra voices share one "Others" label the doctor can split by hand.

    Never interrupt the consultation

    While recording, the screen only counts voices. Naming waits until the doctor stops.

    Sona suggests, the doctor decides

    Sona guesses a role from what the voice said and shows the sentence that made it think so. Nothing is a name until the doctor confirms it.

    Name once, apply everywhere

    Naming a voice labels every line it spoke. Fixing one line moves only that line.

    Colour keeps its meaning

    People are told apart by initials and grey tone, not by hue. Amber stays for "needs you", green for done.

    The note says who reported it

    History from a family member is written as theirs ("daughter reports"), so the doctor can weigh it.

    Open: not built, not hosted

    No voice-separating model runs today. It would be added to box ② in AWS Malaysia. It has not been tested on Malay or Manglish speech.
