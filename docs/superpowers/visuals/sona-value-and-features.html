Sona - Value & How It Works

On-device ambient medical scribe (+ Guardian) · one-page brief

Sona writes the clinical note for the doctor, on their own phone, in their own language. The patient's audio never leaves the device.
Doctors lose about 2 hours a day to paperwork. Every rival scribe is cloud-based, English-first, and useless offline. Sona is on-device, so it is PDPA-clean, works in rural clinics, speaks Malay and Manglish, and costs near zero per minute to run.

Why it matters - 4 reasons

+

Saves time

~47s per note instead of minutes of typing. Hours back each day.

lock

Private by design

Audio and identity stay on the phone. Clears PDPA and data-residency law.

wifi

Offline + multilingual

Works with no signal. Handles Malay, English, and code-switching.

$

Cheap to scale

On-device inference is roughly $0 per minute. Fat margins at scale.

How the scribe works - the pipeline

1

Consent + listen

Doctor taps record. On-device speech-to-text writes a live transcript (Malay / English).

v
2

Redact on device

On-device model strips names and identifiers. The re-identify map stays on the phone.

v
3

Write the note (offline path)

Local model turns the de-identified transcript into a structured SOAP note. Airplane mode still works.

unlock
Only de-identified text may ever leave the device, and only on the optional premium path (cloud model for top-quality notes). no audiono names
4

Review + sign

Names re-inserted locally. Every line traces back to the transcript. Doctor edits and signs.

v
5

Export + audit

FHIR and PDF out to the record. Immutable audit log. Raw audio discarded after transcript.

The features - and how each one works

Core - B2B revenue

Ambient scribe

Listens to the consult and auto-writes a SOAP note plus problem list, medications, and ICD-10 codes. Doctor reviews and signs. This is the paid product.

Assistant

Ask Sona

Hands-free voice query over the patient's own notes, on device. "What was his last HbA1c?" No typing, no cloud.

Guardian - reach + impact

Camera vitals

Points the camera at a face and reads heart rate, breathing, HRV, stress using rPPG, fully on device. Shown as in-range or out-of-range, never as a diagnosis.

Guardian - save a life

Emergency + pill ID

Detects warning signs, gives guided first-response steps, escalates to 999, and identifies a pill from the camera with interaction warnings. Always human-in-the-loop.

Why it can scale - the moats

$
Cost

Local inference is near-free per minute. Cloud rivals bleed GPU and API cost on every consult.

lock
Privacy and law

Audio never leaves the device, so it clears data-residency rules (Malaysia, India, GDPR) that block cloud rivals.

net
Connectivity

Works offline, so it reaches rural and public clinics that cloud scribes cannot serve.

grow
Land and expand

Scribe is the front door. Owns the consented clinical data, then grows into coding, billing, and Guardian.

Who pays

Private clinics

SaaS per seat. Benchmark $50 to $119 per clinician per month.

Public sector (B2G)

MOH clinics and hospitals, on-prem, data stays on national soil.

Expansion

Coding and billing automation, then the consumer Guardian surface.
