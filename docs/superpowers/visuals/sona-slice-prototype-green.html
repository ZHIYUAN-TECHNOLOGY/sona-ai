The AI scribe

Three things happen at once, and none of them touch the cloud

Cloud scribes stream your patient's voice to a server and ask you to trust a privacy policy. Sona does the work where the audio already is.

Hears the room

On-device Whisper transcribes in real time, tags who is speaking, and follows the conversation as it slips between Bahasa Malaysia and English.

Removes the person

Names, IC numbers, phone and address are stripped before any model reads a word. The map that puts them back never leaves the Secure Enclave.

Writes the note

A local model drafts a structured SOAP note with orders and follow-ups. You review, edit and sign. The audio is destroyed the moment you do.

Why it wins

The category leader just proved the thesis

Heidi raised 65 million dollars and shipped a wearable microphone to get audio on-device. The note still generates in their cloud. Ours never needs one.

Heidi, cloud-first
  • Note generation is 100% cloud, even with their wearable
  • Offline mode only queues audio, no note without signal
  • De-identification runs in their cloud, a policy you trust
  • Lost-recording reviews dragged Trustpilot to about 3.3
  • Pricing 40 to 150 US dollars per user monthly, no SEA plan
Sona, on-device
  • The note is born on the phone, airplane mode is the demo
  • Full pipeline offline: transcribe, redact, draft, sign, export
  • Redaction is visible on screen, re-ID map sealed locally
  • No network, no lost recordings, nothing to intercept
  • RM 99 a month, built for MY and SEA clinics under PDPA 2024
