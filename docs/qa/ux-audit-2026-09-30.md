# Clinician app UX audit (30 Sep 2026)

The clinician iPhone app (`apps/native`) was audited at commit `48d4794`. The audit had three parts: three code reviews (answers and Sona, ops and queue pages, shell and tool pages), about 45 screenshots taken cold on the iPhone 18 Pro Max simulator, and a pass at the largest text size. The visual version, with each problem marked on the real screen next to a mock of the fix, is [ux-audit-2026-09-30.html](ux-audit-2026-09-30.html). The dock options are in [../prototypes/sona-dock-options.html](../prototypes/sona-dock-options.html). Open both in a browser.

**Verdict:** the look is consistent, but what the screens say still disagrees. There are 11 high, about 25 medium and about 15 low findings. Discharge, the guarantee letter, Scan and Sona's page answers already meet every rule and are the patterns to copy.

**Status:** fix pass 1 (high items 1–5) and dock option 1 (high item 11, plus the coach mark and the Today notice) were built on 30 Sep. Next is pass 2 (decisions and roles). The plan is under [Fix plan](#fix-plan).

Paths are relative to `apps/native/`, except those that start with `packages/`. Findings come from reading the code unless they are marked **re-checked**. Those were confirmed again after the reviews, either in the code or on the simulator.

## The standard

Every screen is checked against the [UX principles](../design-direction.md#ux-principles-standing-rules-for-every-screen). The scorecard columns are:

- summary first
- action in place
- review, then decide
- one next step
- minimal
- on the design system

Built for operations is judged per role. Generated answers (json-render specs, and later Laya) are held to the same rules as hand-built pages.

The proposed name for this standard is **"glance-to-decision, on-system"**. It has not been adopted yet; that waits for the user's confirmation. It combines these established ideas:

- "Overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand" (Shneiderman), and progressive disclosure.
- Glanceability and situational awareness.
- Actionable insight, and next best action.
- Human in the loop: review before deciding, then a receipt.
- Clinical decision support.
- Efficiency of use, and time to decision.

## High (11)

### Trust: what the screen says must be true

1. **The one-line summary (lead) contradicts the card below it.** Every answer opens with a fixed sentence:
   - Medication says "Nothing can be cleared until each one is confirmed" directly above "All medicine identities are confirmed".
   - X-ray describes a reference radiograph even for the clinician's own image.
   - Evidence says "The evidence is against routine steroids" above "Not enough evidence found".
   - Leads also show for failed or cancelled answers that have no card.
   - In live mode, reasoning's highlighted server line names community-acquired pneumonia as the leading diagnosis for every patient, including Daniel, whose card says there isn't enough history.

   Evidence: `features/encounter/conversation.tsx:79-90`, `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:233,242`, `features/clinical/medication-card.tsx:34`, `features/imaging/index.tsx:57,138`, `features/evidence/index.tsx:63-64`, `features/clinical/reasoning-card.tsx:91-97`, `packages/api/src/models.ts:95`.

   Fix: build the lead from the same state the card shows, and hide it when there is no card. Don't render the server line when its source is a fixture.

2. **"Here's the consultation note drafted…" appears when no note exists.** The card below it says the consultation hasn't been played yet. Evidence: `features/encounter/conversation.tsx:79-90`, `features/scribe/note.tsx:34-42`. Fix: the lead names the real state and the step that changes it.

3. **Several next steps that disagree.**
   - Reasoning ends with its own "Next step" card, then the visit's step ("Amelia is discharged · Hand over"), then "Also ask".
   - The briefing has "Next · … Confirm" at the top and another Confirm at the end.
   - For Daniel, the briefing card says "Start consultation" while the end of the visit says "Brief me".

   Evidence: `features/clinical/index.tsx:52-60`, `features/clinical/reasoning-card.tsx:123-128`, `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:253-260,297`, `features/encounter/visit-next.ts`.

   Fix: answers keep no next step of their own. The end of the visit shows the only one (`threadEnd`), and an answer's suggestion becomes that step when nothing more pressing is open.

4. **Pages count the same thing differently.**
   - Today counts items for "need you" ("5 need you") while Patients and Today's own ✦ answer count patients ("2 need you").
   - In demo mode the Ward shows Bed 6 with 3 blockers while its Discharge says 5, because the two X-ray findings are missing from the demo blockers. Today also shows those findings before any X-ray is read.
   - Ward numbers on Today's key, the sidebar and the ✦ "soonest" answer come from the demo ward, while the Ward page uses server beds.
   - The task badge and sidebar count only tasks made on the device.
   - Patients keeps flagging the demo potassium result after the real one is acknowledged.

   Evidence: `features/day/index.tsx:288,312`, `features/encounter/patient-screen.tsx:55-58,64-79`, `features/ask/today.tsx:17-23`, `features/live/findings-sync.ts:14`, `features/discharge/index.tsx:169-171`, `packages/domain/src/ward.ts:56-67`, `features/ward/index.tsx:59-60`, `features/encounter/quick-actions.ts:35,60,77`, `features/encounter/sidebar.tsx:70,76,98`, `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:268`, `features/encounter/patient-sheet.tsx:40`.

   Fix: one hook each for today's visits, the ward beds, "needs you" and open tasks, read by every page. Patients takes the result from the same place Today does.

5. **"New result to review" never goes away.** After the briefing, the visit still says "New result to review · Brief me". The briefing never shows the result, so this loop has no exit. Evidence: `features/encounter/quick-actions.ts:105`, `features/day/model.ts:32`, `features/clinical/index.tsx:40-54`, `packages/api/src/models.ts:90-94`, `features/encounter/visit-next.ts:13`. Fix: the briefing leads with the result and an Acknowledge button, and the receipt says who acknowledged it.

### Decisions: review before deciding, every time

6. **Prescribing can run without the card's review.**
   - "✦ Run the prescribing simulation" sits in "Also ask" (hint: "Asks Sona") but starts prescribing, which creates a receipt if checks pass.
   - The next-step bar says step 3 of 5 while the card says "Resolve 5". The bar leaves out the indication, regimen, coverage and prerequisite checks.
   - **Re-checked in code:** in live mode the bar's Run calls the local fixture. It skips the server and the doctor-only check, so a nurse can simulate from the bar.
   - A recorded risk (for example "Yes" to liver disease) reads "Resolve 1 to prescribe". The only way to resolve it is to flip the answer.
   - Nothing next to the button says it creates an immutable receipt.

   Evidence: `features/encounter/conversation.tsx:45,51-53`, `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:163-166`, `features/encounter/store.tsx:92-95,101-112`, `features/clinical/prescribe-card.tsx:130-138,160-163,176,206,209`, `features/encounter/next-steps.ts:20-28`, `packages/domain/src/prescribing.ts:40`.

   Fix:
   - Questions stay questions: remove prescription from `askAfter`.
   - The bar runs through the card's `run`, with the role lock, and builds its steps from every blocked check.
   - A recorded risk reads "Outside the supported case · not prescribing".
   - A one-line consequence sits under the enabled button.

7. **Opening a result acknowledges it for everyone.** In live mode, tapping "Most urgent: Low potassium" or the Results key clears the result for the whole team before anyone has read it. If the result is critical, it also silently clears the critical banner and skips its receipt. This was a deliberate earlier choice; the audit recommends reversing it. Evidence: `features/day/index.tsx:257-261`, `features/encounter/quick-actions.ts:80-82`; compare `features/critical/index.tsx:75-85`. Fix: opening shows the result; acknowledging is its own button, with a receipt.

8. **An urgent patient message can be swiped away.** One right swipe marks an urgent thread handled, with no confirmation and no undo, and the red row leaves Today too. Evidence: `features/inbox/index.tsx:94-106,178-186`. Fix: urgent rows ask once before closing without a reply, and every swipe gets a 5-second Undo, like the handover deck.

9. **Typing in the note marks the medication review stale.** **Re-checked in code:** each keystroke calls `updateEncounter(…, true)`. That revises the encounter, clears the medication review and cancels amendments. The prescribing card then says "Needs rerun", the bar drops back to "Run medication review", and every earlier answer gets an "earlier revision" notice. Evidence: `features/scribe/note.tsx:145-149,192`, `features/encounter/state.ts:43-44`, `features/clinical/prescribe-card.tsx:151,175`, `features/encounter/next-steps.ts:26`, `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:244`. Fix: note edits don't revise the encounter, because the note doesn't feed the checks.

### Flow and roles: each role gets a page it can act on

10. **A bed manager gets the doctor's Today.**
    - The first item is "Confirm Amelia's medicines".
    - The Results key shows a demo potassium result the role can't read. The refused load falls back to demo data.
    - Hand over opens "Bed managers can't read the handover".

    Evidence: `features/day/index.tsx:241`, `features/encounter/quick-actions.ts:54,86-90`, `features/live/server-day.ts:26-31`, `packages/api/src/roles.ts:65-69`.

    Fix: a ward-first Today for bed managers (blocked beds, owners, one-tap assign). Hide keys the role can't use, and handle each refused call on its own.

11. **Today's dock shows visit-only tools.** **Re-checked on the simulator:** the + menu and the X-ray, recorder and note buttons appear on Today, where there is no visit. A photo attached there can't be sent ("Pick a visit first…") and stays stuck above the mic. Evidence: `features/encounter/workspace-screen.tsx:184,286-290`, `features/encounter/voice-dock.tsx:196,266,306-310,318`. Fix: pass the tool handlers only inside a visit, like every other page.

### Colour means something

- **"Blocked" has two colours.** It is red on the Ward page, its bars and the bed card, but amber on Today's Ward key. The principles say amber.
- **Insights are red when their items are amber.** "2 need you" on Patients is red when both flags are amber, and Handover's "2 to watch" is red over amber chips.
- **Calm tool tiles are blue,** though blue means the next step. The amber disc uses an off-token `#B7741E`.
- **Key terms are tinted by category, not meaning.** Diagnoses are green and symptoms blue. "Severe pneumonia" is red in general claims about a non-severe patient, and done ticks are blue in some places and green in others.
- Evidence: `features/ward/ops.tsx:29-30,133`, `features/ward/index.tsx:209,254`, `features/encounter/quick-actions.ts:92`, `features/encounter/patient-screen.tsx:79`, `features/handover/index.tsx:58-59,164`, `components/sona/quick-actions.tsx:25-30,69,131,149`, `components/sona/marked-text.tsx:15-21`, `components/sona/marked-terms.ts:18,28,42,58`.
- Fix: one mapping, applied everywhere, with the insight coloured by its most urgent item. The mapping is red (must not miss), amber (soon or blocked), green (done or safe), blue (the one next step) and neutral (everything else).

## Fix plan

Four passes, trust first. Each assumes one agent in `apps/native`, with tests and a simulator check after the pass.

1. **Trust (about half a day)**
   - Leads built from card state.
   - One next step per visit.
   - One source for every count.
   - The result shown in the briefing, with Acknowledge.
2. **Decisions and roles (about half a day)**
   - The prescribing bar runs through the card's checks.
   - Acknowledge only after review.
   - Confirm and Undo for urgent swipes.
   - Note edits stop revising the visit.
   - Role-aware Today, Tasks and Handover.
3. **Coverage and access (about half a day)**
   - Sona on the remaining pages, and the orb beside the Note and Scan decisions.
   - One colour mapping everywhere.
   - Large text, tap targets and the VoiceOver escapes.
   - Sample labels in demo mode.
   - Dock tools only in a visit, and the coach mark once.
4. **Polish (about 2 hours)**
   - Working press animations.
   - The remaining hand-set styles.
   - Dead code, stale labels and the blank Discharge and guarantee letter.

## The dock: five options, ranked

The current dock stacks three layers at the bottom of every page: the floating ✦ suggestion, the coach mark and the dock. They overlap content, clip at large text, and show visit-only tools on Today. The options were scored 1 to 5 on each criterion. Reaching the suggestion and acting on it count double, because the goal is the fastest route from a glance to a decision. The maximum is 45.

| Rank | Option | Reach ×2 | Act ×2 | Minimal | Fits direction | Voice | Large text | Effort | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Suggestion in the dock** (recommended) | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **43** |
| 2 | Today's dock, refined | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 35 |
| 3 | Next action and Sona | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 34 |

1. **Suggestion in the dock.** The dock itself carries the page's ✦ suggestion. Tap it to see the answer, tap ⌨ to type, or tap or hold the orb to talk. There is one layer at the bottom, the suggestion is always in the same place, and it keeps the approved orb and hold-to-talk. The costs: the suggestion truncates on narrow phones, and typing is one tap further away. It borrows one idea from option 3: a suggestion can be an action ("Assign billing to Bed 2"), which always opens a review before anything happens. About half a day.
2. **Today's dock, refined.** Keep the layout, add a fade band, pin the ✦ chip inside the band, and show the coach mark once per install. It is the smallest change (about 2 hours), but it still has two layers, and the chip can wrap at large text.
3. **Next action and Sona.** The bottom bar shows the page's next best action with a Review button, with the orb beside it, as the review pages already do. It is the fastest route to a decision, but it repeats the page's feature card, some pages have no single next action, and it takes 1–2 days.
4. **Text-first composer.** A full-width field with a mic, and suggestion chips above it. It is familiar, but it drops the approved orb and hold-to-talk, puts a second text field back on Tasks, and brings back two layers.
5. **Tabs with Sona in the centre.** Four tabs with the orb in the middle, like Sona Care. It duplicates the approved grouped sidebar, tabs would differ by role, and it crowds at large text.

These stay the same in every option:

- The orb means "talk to or ask Sona", and ✦ marks only a suggestion.
- Review pages (Discharge, the guarantee letter, X-ray) keep the orb beside the decision.
- Answers open in the page's Sona sheet, with its text box at the bottom.
- The + and the visit tools show only inside a visit, on every page including Today.
- A soft fade sits behind the bottom bar.
