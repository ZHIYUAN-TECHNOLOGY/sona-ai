Sona golden paths

Manual test checklist for the iPhone UI, updated for the demo flow: ranked day, section sources, Next-step bar, medicine thumbnails, camera scan and shift handover (25 September 2026).

iPhone Release build · ai.zhiyuantech.sonaSimulator · Expo GoSession-only data
Start: open Sona (force-quit first for a fresh session). Home shows "Here’s what needs you first." and Your day, ranked. Paths 3–5 and 7–12 run inside a visit session opened in path 2. Every answer opens with Takeaways (up to three lines marked !, + or i); each section has a tinted icon header and, on its right, one N sources pill instead of inline links. Key terms are tinted (blue symptom, violet medicine, amber caution, teal finding). Demo-only controls (scenario picker, "Load synthetic eligibility", failure simulations) are hidden until you turn on Settings › Show demo controls in answers.

13Shift handover

Home › "Hand over shift" · or sidebar › Shift handover

  1. Open the handover."5 of 5 left" and a card stack. Each card is SBAR: S, B, A lines and R open items drawn from the visit (e.g. "Act on low potassium", "Confirm 2 reported medicines", "Review and sign the consultation note"), a Watch or Stable chip and a sources pill. Watch patients first; ward patients are marked "synthetic ward".
  2. Drag the top card right slowly, then let go past the middle.It tilts and tints teal (Handed over), flies off, the next card scales up; the counter reads 4 of 5 and Undo appears.
  3. Drag one card left, or tap Discuss.Amber Discuss verbally. Tap Undo to bring a card back.
  4. Finish the stack with Hand over.A summary: "N handed over · N to discuss", the names to talk through, and an editable Receiving clinician.
  5. Press and hold Hold to accept handover (let go early once).Releasing early springs the fill back. Holding 1.5 s fills it teal and a Handover acknowledged receipt names who accepted how many patients and when.
