Sona golden paths

Manual test checklist for the iPhone UI, updated for the demo flow: day header and ruler, quick keys, ranked day, one-card prescribing, camera scan and shift handover (26 September 2026).

iPhone Release build · ai.zhiyuantech.sonaSimulator · Expo GoSession-only data
Start: open Sona (force-quit first for a fresh session). Home opens on the greeting, today’s date line, the 24-hour ruler, four quick keys and the ranked list. Paths 3–5 and 7–12 run inside a visit session opened in path 2. Every answer opens with Takeaways (up to three lines marked !, + or i); each section has a tinted icon header and, on its right, one N sources pill instead of inline links. Key terms are tinted (blue symptom, violet medicine, amber caution, teal finding). Demo-only controls (scenario picker, "Load synthetic eligibility", failure simulations) are hidden until you turn on Settings › Show demo controls in answers. Demo visit times follow the clock and re-time themselves if the app sits open.

1Home: your day, ranked

Home · first thing on launch

  1. Open Sona."Good afternoon" (or morning/evening), Today, Fri 25 Sep ▾ with "3 need you · 2 visits". Below: the 24-hour ruler, four raised glass keys (a halftone shimmer sweeps across them once), then the Most urgent card (Low potassium · Daniel Rahman, "2 h · no action yet") and the ranked list, each row with its reason.
  2. Look at the ruler, then drag it left and let go.A blue now pill in its own lane; visits as avatar pins with first name and time (two at the same time share one pin: "2 patients · 18:15"). The ruler glides and snaps to the nearest visit or back to now with a tick; dragged away, the now pill pins to the edge with an arrow. Tap it to return.
  3. Tap the grouped pin.A sheet lists both patients with time and status; tap one to open that visit.
  4. Press and hold a key, then release (try Results).The key sinks 2 pt and its ledge flattens, the glyph pulses, one haptic. Results is red with a count; tapping opens Daniel’s briefing. Patients opens the patient list; Hand over opens the handover; Tasks opens follow-ups.
  5. Scroll the home page down.The keys collapse into a slim frosted capsule under the top bar (2 · 1 · ⇄ · ☑), still tappable.
  6. Tap the date line.A month calendar drops from it (visit dots, Today ring). Pick another day: the header reads "Coming up" or "Looking back", the ruler opens on that day’s first visit, the list shows that day (synthetic, read-only). Today returns.
  7. On today, tap ⋯ on a row, then Snooze 1 h; tap Snoozed · 1, then Wake.The row leaves the list and collects under Snoozed; Wake returns it. Pin to top moves a row above the rest.
  8. Tap the Confirm Amelia’s medicines row.Amelia’s session opens straight into the medication answer with its Next-step bar (path 9).

3Voice orb

Inside a session · bottom centre

  1. Press and hold the orb.An aurora glass wave rises, words appear as if heard, and the orb shows listening bars.
  2. Release.The wave floods up and sinks away, the question sends, and the orb's icon changes (searching, reading) while the answer loads.
  3. Tap the orb once, then tap it again.Hands-free listening starts; the second tap sends.

9Medication review and one-card prescribing

Tap the Medicines key in a visit · or the "Confirm Amelia’s medicines" row in Your day

  1. Open the answer.Takeaways, pinned tabs Medicines · Interactions · Prescribing, "Confirm what’s being taken" 0 of 2 with dashed medicine outlines, and a glass Next step · 1/5 bar with Confirm.
  2. Tap Confirm in the bar and save each medicine.The form opens in place; on save the outline turns away and the drawn medicine springs in with a blue check (salbutamol becomes a blue inhaler). The bar moves to Record eligibility.
  3. Tap Record in the bar.The page glides to the Prescribing card: a progress ring ("6"), the paracetamol regimen, and only the blocking checks, numbered, each with its fix in place: a weight field, No / Yes pills for allergy, liver, kidney and alcohol, and the medication review last. Passed checks fold into "N checks passed ›".
  4. Type 62 for weight, then tap No on each history row.Each fixed row’s number crossfades into a teal check, a line draws through its label, then it folds away; the ring fills and "N left" counts down, one haptic each.
  5. Read the last row."Current medication review · Needs rerun · eligibility or medicines changed since the last review" with Rerun review, and a disabled Resolve 1 to prescribe.
  6. Tap Rerun review.It runs in place (no new answer). The card turns Ready to prescribe · 11 checks passed and the button becomes Prescribe paracetamol.
  7. Tap Prescribe paracetamol."Checking all 11…", then Prescribed · simulation with the stamped receipt summary in the same card; the bar reads All steps done. Earlier prescribing answers show a two-line summary of what they decided.
  8. Optional: tap Prescribe before resolving anything by asking "Run the prescribing simulation" in a fresh visit.The answer’s card shows what blocks it (for example 5 of 11) with the same inline fixes: the AI stops itself until it is safe.

13Shift handover

  1. Open the handover."5 of 5 left" and a card stack. Each card is SBAR: S, B, A lines and R open items drawn from the visit (e.g. "Act on low potassium", "Confirm 2 reported medicines", "Review and sign the consultation note"), a Watch or Stable chip and a sources pill. Watch patients first; ward patients are marked "synthetic ward".
  2. Drag the top card right slowly, then let go past the middle.It tilts and tints teal (Handed over), flies off, the next card scales up; the counter reads 4 of 5 and Undo appears.
  3. Drag one card left, or tap Discuss.Amber Discuss verbally. Tap Undo to bring a card back.
  4. Finish the stack with Hand over.A summary: "N handed over · N to discuss", the names to talk through, and an editable Receiving clinician.
  5. Press and hold Hold to accept handover (let go early once).Releasing early springs the fill back. Holding 1.5 s fills it teal and a Handover acknowledged receipt names who accepted how many patients and when.
