2 · Decisions

Review before deciding, every time

These gaps let something happen before the person has seen what it will do.

HighReview before deciding

"Also ask" can run prescribing, and the step counts don't match

"Run the prescribing simulation" sits in the questions list but performs prescribing. The bar says step 3 of 5 while the card says "Resolve 5". In live mode the bar also skips the server and the doctor-only check (confirmed in code).

Fix: questions stay questions. The bar counts what's left, like the card, and runs through the card's own checks, with a lock for roles that can't prescribe.

Also found

Medium and low, in short

Should fix

  • Typing in the note marks the medication review stale ("Needs rerun") and adds "earlier revision" notices.
  • Sona is missing on Messages, Handover, Coding audit and Training, and beside the Note and Scan decisions.
  • VoiceOver can get stuck in the next-step sheet and the patient sheet; about 20 targets are under 44 pt.
  • Answers show 2–3 highlighted cards, duplicate receipts in prescribing, and errors with no Retry.
  • Demo mode drops "Sample" labels on the discharge summary, leaflet, guarantee letter codes and huddle.

Flow

  • Back and close controls are mixed across modals.
  • Opening a visit from Patients loses the tab you were on.
  • Snoozed items never come back; the critical alert has no next step after Acknowledge.
  • The handover receipt counts "Discuss" patients as accepted.
  • Tasks can't be reassigned or redated, and have no role check.

Polish

  • Press animations: about 57 transitions sit on plain components that ignore them, so presses snap.
  • Hand-set styles left: 142 text sizes, 56 hex colours, about 52 corner sizes.
  • Dead code: orb.tsx, ResponseFrame's pending branch, old helpers.
  • Discharge and the guarantee letter open blank with no close button from a link without a visit.
  • Stale labels: menu "History and settings", route /history, fake 250 ms loading on Source.
Fix plan

Four passes, trust first

Estimates assume one agent in apps/native, with tests and a simulator check after each pass. Not verified yet: a physical iPhone, VoiceOver, frame rate, and the nurse, bed-manager and management roles on a device.

Trust

About half a day

  • One source for every count
  • Result shown with Acknowledge
