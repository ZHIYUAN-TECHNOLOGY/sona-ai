Ambient scribe: consultation to coded note

How a spoken consultation in any mix of English, Malay and Mandarin becomes a reviewed note with ICD-10 codes. “Record visit” and “12 s parts” are drawn as one step.

Measured, synthetic only

  • 20 synthetic consultations of 2–8 people (tools/diar-eval): 97.1% of turns go to the right person with 12 s parts and memory handed on
  • Real rooms with several real people are not yet scored
  • Sona Speech keeps nothing between parts; a part with no speech keeps the last memory

What the checks drop

  • A statement citing nothing in range, or with a number or medicine not in the transcript
  • An assessment or plan resting only on patient or family sentences; draft and turns logged to provenance
  • A code whose evidence is not a note sentence id, or not in icd10_codes

Known limits

  • Web has no recorder; it drafts from a pasted transcript
  • json_object holds valid JSON, not a schema; shape is checked in code
  • One real voice can be heard as two; the doctor merges them
