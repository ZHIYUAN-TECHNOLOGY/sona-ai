Sona Care check-ins and care-team tasks

What happens after discharge, and how a patient's reply becomes work for the care team. Booking in creates the appointment, completes the task and pushes booked.

Agent decisions (decideFollowUp)

  • Re-ask the latest check-in when it has had no reply for 2–24 h
  • After a worse reply, bring a check-in forward to tomorrow if none is due by then
  • Replies older than 24 h are logged, not acted on; timeline keeps the last 40 entries
  • Ticks come from every reply, the hourly cron (0 * * * *), a demo-clock jump and checkIns.tick

Demo clock

  • hospitals.clock_offset_ms shifts “now” for every route
  • demo.jump (demo hospital only) moves to day 1, a check-in day, the review visit or the agent's next action
  • Then it wakes follow-ups and announces demo to every linked patient; Sona Care reads it with care.clock

Known limits

  • Tasks are always owned by “Attending doctor”; nurses can list and complete them
  • A repeat of the same open task title is reused; urgent posts 999 in the care-team thread once
  • No push token: the check-in still waits in the app; the re-ask is logged
