Where patient data goes

What leaves the phone, what each model sees, what is stored and where it lives. Staging holds synthetic patients only; patient data is not resident in Malaysia today.

Not kept

Audio never touches storage

  • The Worker decodes each base64 part in memory; sona-voice diarizes and transcribes from BytesIO. Neither writes it.
  • The phone deletes each part after a successful transcribe; a part that failed twice stays on the phone for Retry.
  • Library scans (library.readScan): pages are not saved, only the staff-checked text (library.addScan).

Kept, worth knowing

Most model calls get text as written

  • Transcript turns are stored: the draft and its turns go to provenance.output. Notes keep one row per revision.
  • As written: scribe transcript, note sentences for codes, appeals (with the name), triage and replies (first name), check-in replies, care answers. Images go as pixels.
  • De-identified only for evidence questions (name, name parts, MRN), training samples (rules, OpenMed on sona-pii, leak gate) and scanned ward notes (name text only).
  • The X-ray library document carries the patient's name and id; evidence passages are not de-identified.
  • Leaves Sona: the claim (codes with their note sentences, frozen at send) is shown to the insurer through its link. The 999 push includes the first name.

Residency today

Not resident in Malaysia yet

  • Models: Malaysia; no model call leaves for a US host while SAGEMAKER_ENDPOINTS names every box.
  • Postgres: Neon, Singapore. Worker and R2: Cloudflare.
  • No retention job: nothing deletes R2 objects or old rows except the demo reset.
  • Staging holds synthetic patients only.
