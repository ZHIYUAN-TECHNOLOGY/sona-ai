Sona Care live voice call

How a patient talks to Sona hands-free, and how a warning sign reaches the care team. Three models in Malaysia: ASR and TTS on sona-voice, Gemma on sona-brain.

What streams, what does not

  • ASR is per turn, not streaming: the energy VAD starts a turn after 300 ms of loud frames and ends it after 500 ms quiet or at 30 s; the whole turn is transcribed
  • Gemma streams; its first word is the triage label, then sentences cut as they arrive
  • TTS streams each sentence back as 48 kHz PCM; a sentence not ready in 8 s (16 s after the first) is read by the phone's own voice, labelled

Safety and identity

  • The Worker names the agent hospital/patient from the care link, never from the path
  • A red-flag word (English, Malay, Chinese) speaks the 999 line before Gemma is asked
  • The task is created or reused (owner “Attending doctor”) before Sona says she told the care team

Measured, staging, 1 Oct

  • Warm: heard 1.5 s after the patient stopped, first words 2.3 s, first audio 3.5 s
  • First call after a deploy was cold: 17.7 s
  • Echo gate and cut-in constants still to be tuned on a physical iPhone
