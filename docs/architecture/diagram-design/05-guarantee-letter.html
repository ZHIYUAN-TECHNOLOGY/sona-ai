Guarantee letter to discharge

The states a claim moves through (claims.status), who moves it, and what blocks sending and discharge. Staging, synthetic insurer: no real payer integration.

Send checklist

Send is refused until

  • A severity score is documented (CURB-65, CRB-65, qSOFA, NEWS).
  • An imaging result is attached; an order such as “X-ray to check for pneumonia” does not count.
  • Pneumonia: the diagnosis is confirmed (“possible” or “to rule out” does not count, and the doctor can confirm it only once the visit's X-ray is read) and the organism is documented.
  • A reason for IV antibiotics is given when IV is mentioned.
  • A doctor approves. Criteria are read on the server against the saved note, the X-ray as Sona Vision read it and the chart's CURB-65.

Discharge readiness

Discharge is refused until

  • Vitals within limits: temp ≤ 37.8, pulse ≤ 100, resp ≤ 24, systolic ≥ 90, SpO₂ ≥ 90.
  • GL received (stays with a GL), medicines confirmed, follow-up chosen.
  • No open finding, no pending lab or transport wait. A check appears only when the record can answer it.
  • discharge.request (doctor only) then creates tasks, schedules check-ins, sets dischargedAt, approves the leaflet and starts the check-in agent.

Link page and limits

One decision per link

  • Any other transition gets “Already decided”; an expired link is refused.
  • The insurer's POST announces claim on HospitalChannel; the letter page updates within a second.
  • No amount is sent (estimateCents = 0). A self-pay stay has no GL row and no GL check.
  • Link expiry uses real time while sentAt uses the demo clock.
