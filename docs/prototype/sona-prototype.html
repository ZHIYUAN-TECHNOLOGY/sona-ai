The pipeline (every step on the phone):
consult audio→ Whisper STT→ OpenMed de-identify→ Qwen2.5 SOAP + paperwork→ re-identify (local)→ doctor signs→ PDF · FHIR · CMS · audit→ audio discarded

Why it wins: the only scribe that runs fully offline, keeps every byte of PHI on the device, speaks rojak, and costs ~RM0 per consult. Cloud incumbents can't follow without rebuilding their architecture. Full feature list & business model: see docs/sona-feature-roadmap.md.
