# Sheets and modals audit, Sona Pro and Sona Care (6 Oct 2026)

Read-only audit of `main` at `e106023`. Nothing was changed or run; every finding comes from reading the code. Two behaviours marked "check on device" depend on how iOS stacks things and should be confirmed in the simulator before fixing.

Paths are relative to `apps/native` (Sona Pro) or `apps/care` (Sona Care).

## 1. Summary

- **Sona Pro: 20 sheets or modals.** 16 are routes in `app/_layout.tsx` (6 form sheets, 8 modals, 1 full-screen camera, plus the patient card pushed inside the Patients sheet). 4 are drawn inside screens (2 React Native page sheets, 2 custom sheets). There are also 5 other overlays that are fine as they are (side menu, ward bed card, X-ray `⋯` menu, critical result banner, system alerts).
- **Sona Care: 9 sheets or modals.** 3 are routes in `app/_layout.tsx` (1 modal, 2 form sheets). 6 are custom white sheets drawn in screens (5 React Native `Modal`s, 1 overlay).
- **13 nested chains found**: 10 in Sona Pro, 3 in Sona Care. A "chain" is anything opening on top of a sheet or modal that is still open.
- **What to do**:

| | Full page | Keep as sheet | In place | Keep as is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sona Pro (20) | 10 | 7 | 2 | 1 (full-screen camera) |
| Sona Care (9) | 4 | 5 | 0 | 0 |

  There are also 4 in-place fixes for links that live inside other surfaces: the patient card's "Full history & sources", the appeal's "From the note", the "Sample" and "AI draft" labels inside review pages, and the language choices inside Care's Settings.
- **The pattern behind most chains**: Sona Pro's review pages (Discharge, Guarantee letter, X-ray, Scan, Consultation, Settings) open as modals, and they open more things: another modal, a page, a source sheet, or the "About data" sheet. Making those pages normal pushed pages removes 8 of the 10 Pro chains at once. In Care, Settings is a sheet whose rows open their own sheets.
- **Signs of the problem in the code**: several hand-offs wait on timers so that one sheet can close before the next thing opens: `components/sona/sources.tsx:121` (350 ms), `features/discharge/index.tsx:240` (320 ms), `features/library/scanned-sheet.tsx:37` (300 ms), `features/encounter/sidebar.tsx:69` (120 ms). Each one marks a place where one surface is trying to hand over to another.

## 4. The rule used

1. **Keep as a sheet** only if it is short (fits without scrolling), does one thing (a pick, a confirm, a short explainer, a menu) and the person is done in a few seconds with the page behind still useful.
2. **Make it a full page** if it is long, has steps, has keyboard fields, uses the camera, or is somewhere people stay to read, review or decide.
3. **Make it in place** if it is a small detail of something already on screen (a citation, what a label means, a photo to compare). It unfolds right there, so the answer lands in the page.
4. **Nothing opens on top of a sheet or a modal.** If a sheet's content needs to open more, the sheet closes first, or the surface should be a page.
5. A sheet over a page is fine, and so is a page pushed from a page. System alerts, pickers, share sheets and the full-screen camera are fine anywhere.
