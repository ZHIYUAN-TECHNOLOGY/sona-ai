### Sona — 5-Day On-Device Demo Build

Turn the Sona scribe prototype into **one flow that genuinely runs on-device, in airplane mode**, on a single iPhone 17 Pro Max: record a consult, watch the note appear with every identifier stripped on the phone, sign, export. That live run is the competition weapon and it proves the moat (0 bytes of PHI leave the device). Target completion: end of Day 5 (~2026-07-09).

Depth over breadth. We build ~6 real screens as clean cross-platform code that the public app grows from; the other ~17 prototype screens, Guardian, the web dashboard, and the cloud control-plane stay in the prototype and deck as the roadmap.

<Callout id="scope-lock" tone="decision">

**Scope is locked to the on-device slice.** 5 days can buy real-on-device-moat OR full breadth, not both. We build the moat as the product foundation; breadth + the tiered cloud fallback for weak phones is the immediate roadmap right after.

</Callout>
