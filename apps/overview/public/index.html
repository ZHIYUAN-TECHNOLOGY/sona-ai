Since the preliminary round

We moved Sona's AI from the phone to the hospital's private server.

In September we planned Sona to run fully on the phone. Building it taught us that a hospital needs something else.

September planSmall AI models on each phone. Zero cloud.
NOW
What we builtFull-size models on a private server the hospital controls: its own server room, or its private cloud in Malaysia. Today they run on our private servers in Malaysia.
Same promiseLocal models. Patient data stays in Malaysia. No outside AI company sees it.
  1. Every phone gets the same Sona.A RM1,000 phone runs only a small 2B model. The scribe needs a 26B one. On a server, the oldest phone on the ward gets the same answer as the newest.
  2. A bigger model gives safer answers.We tested it: our best 8B model scored 8 to 16 points lower on medical exams than the 26B model Sona uses now.
  3. Patient data stays in one locked place.Not on hundreds of personal phones that get lost or shared.
  4. Hospital work is shared work.A letter, the ward board and a call from home all need the same record at the same moment.
  5. Phones last a 12-hour shift.No battery drain from running AI. IT updates one server, not every phone.

Still private: every AI model runs in Malaysia, and no patient text goes to an outside AI company.

Private by design

Patient data never leaves Malaysia for an AI company.

Under PDPA 2024 a breach means mandatory notice and fines up to RM1 million. Local models are how a hospital uses AI and stays compliant.

Every model in Malaysia

All ten run in AWS Malaysia (ap-southeast-5, Kuala Lumpur) on servers we control. No patient text is sent to an outside AI company.

Names stripped first

Sona Privacy removes names, IC numbers and phone numbers before anything is saved for training, saved from a scan, or searched outside, like PubMed.

Audio not kept

Consultation audio is transcribed in memory and not stored. The note and transcript stay in the record.

A person approves

Every AI output is a draft until staff approve it. Nothing leaves for an insurer or a patient without a person.

Roles and access log

Doctors, nurses, bed managers and management each see what their role needs. Every access is logged.

Safety without AI

If a model is down, safety still runs on rules: red-flag words always get the 999 line and a care-team task.

Sona Privacy at work: the patient chose to share. Her doctor's note becomes a training sample with her name replaced by [NAME] before anything is kept, and a search for her name finds nothing.

Every AI model runs in Malaysia. Real patient data will only ever be stored in Malaysia.
